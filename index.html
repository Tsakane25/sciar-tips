Sciar Tips · for restaurant owners · alpha

Card tips paid to your team every Monday, and every person can see how theirs was worked out.

Sciar Tips connects to your card terminal, splits the week's card tips by the rule you set, and pays your staff each Monday. Here is what our twelve-week pilot with one Dublin restaurant shows.

Three things that are true today

  1. €31,754.13 in card tips came in over the 12-week pilot, and €31,754.09 was paid out to staff. The 4 cent difference is rounding.1
  2. 12 Mondays, 152 payouts Staff were paid every week of the pilot, one payout per person per week.2
  3. Every payout is explained Each person can open their own staff view to see their hours, which hours earned tips, and the sum behind their payout, and can write a question to Sciar. Their colleagues' pay stays private. (In this alpha, questions are saved on the person's own phone and not yet sent.)3
